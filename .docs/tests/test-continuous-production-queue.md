# Continuous production queue E2E

1. With a fresh eight-page story and controlled provider, submit production demand at page zero. POST returns before provider work finishes. Hold first image: no later image starts. Release it: up to three later images start. Complete one while its siblings stay held: its image appears in session GET and another eligible page starts immediately. Buffer ends at page six; page one demand enables page seven.
2. With two stories and multiple viewers, hold provider calls and verify global configured image cap. Release one viewer, expire another, and delete a story: no new paid jobs for inactive/deleted stories, other viewers continue, and already-started results remain saved. Restart before old viewer TTL expiry: no paid dispatch until fresh demand. Reopen after a process restart: successful outputs are reused; interrupted jobs are explicitly failed until retry.
3. Fail local publication after successful image generation. Retry through both production and legacy advance after fixing local failure: provider call count stays unchanged and saved image is published once, with one usage entry. Fail a paid image: no further paid dispatch until bounded explicit/automatic retry, and sibling successes persist.
4. Run a job across its renewal interval and issue a legacy advance concurrently: no duplicate paid call or stale lease takeover. Stop application during work: stop dispatch, retain settled outputs, drain publication, then close database without unhandled rejections.
5. Register synthetic voice, processing, motion and video assembly handlers with distinct pools and dependencies. Voice and images can progress independently after script; assembly starts only after required artifacts succeed. Failed assembly retry reuses assets and never reruns successful jobs. Version changes create different job identities.
6. In the in-app browser against controlled local providers, open an existing generated story, observe first and later images update, navigate before the old three-page boundary, leave/reopen, and confirm continued progress with saved assets. Record browser/session and avoid claiming live-provider throughput.
