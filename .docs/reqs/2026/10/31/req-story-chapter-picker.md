# Story or chapter picker

Readers who know what they want should choose an original story or chapter instead of waiting for theme discovery. Add a direct source choice beside the existing discovery path.

- [ ] Selecting a bundled book immediately lists its stories/chapters; the new-story sheet also offers theme discovery and direct story/chapter selection for bundled books, with searchable source titles, chapter context, loading, retry and empty states.
- [ ] Named Liaozhai stories and individual Shishuo anecdotes are selectable; other books expose their parsed chapters. Entries retain exact source ranges and edition. Unreadable or oversized choices cannot be submitted.
- [ ] Direct selection persists one selected candidate and prepares its verified original/count without AI discovery. Production still requires explicit final confirmation; replay creates no duplicate task.
- [ ] Book/mode changes clear obsolete source choices; late responses cannot restore another book's options. Existing theme discovery and custom-book setup keep working.
- [ ] Source validation rejects stale, forged and cross-book choices before persistence. Browser verification uses isolated data and controlled generation; no paid inference or packaging.

Non-goals: redesigning reading navigation, parsing new editions, manually editing source ranges, or changing queue/schema contracts.
